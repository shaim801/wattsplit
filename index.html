What it is

One meter on the wall. Several homes paying for it.

A calculator built for shared prepaid meters.

When flats share one main meter that everyone tops up together, someone has to work out who owes what. WattSplit does that maths in seconds.

The main meter

Tracks the total kWh and balance spent since the last recharge.

Your sub-meter

Records only what your flat actually used.

How it works

Four readings. One fair number.

1

Note the readings after recharge

Write down the main meter reading and balance, plus every sub-meter reading.

2

Wait until the balance runs low

Use electricity as normal until it's time for the next top-up.

3

Note them all again

Record the main balance, main reading and each sub-meter reading once more.

4

Enter them in WattSplit

Get the rate per kWh and your exact share of the bill instantly.

Money spent = Previous balance − Current balance
Rate per kWh = Money spent ÷ Main meter kWh used
Your bill = Your kWh used × Rate per kWh
Example: ৳1,275.83 ÷ 219.39 kWh = ৳5.8154 per kWh
26.74 kWh × ৳5.8154 = ৳155.50

FAQ

Questions people ask.

What kind of meter does this work with?

A prepaid main meter shared by several flats, each with its own sub-meter. It works whether the readings count up or count down.

Why does the rate change every cycle?

Tariff slabs, demand charges and VAT mean the effective ৳ per kWh moves around. WattSplit recalculates it from what you really paid.

What if the sub-meters don't add up to the main meter?

You'll see a warning with the difference. It's often common lights, a water pump or line loss, but it can also mean a reading was copied wrong.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.
